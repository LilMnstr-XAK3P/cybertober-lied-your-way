Accessibility

How this site works for everyone

This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, the standard used for school websites under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

  • Every page, game and quiz works with a keyboard alone. Use Tab to move, Enter or Space to choose, and Esc to close a window. The word search uses the arrow keys.
  • Every poster has a “Read the poster text” button with its full words, so screen readers can read it.
  • Videos open with captions turned on. You can also turn captions on or off with the CC button in the player.
  • Text and buttons meet AA color contrast. The site works when zoomed to 200% and on phones, without sideways scrolling.
  • Animations stop on their own and turn off if your device is set to reduce motion.

Content from other websites. Some videos and PDF handouts come from other organizations, such as CISA, NIST and the National Cybersecurity Alliance. We cannot change those files. If one of them is hard to use, ask your teacher for another format.

Something not working for you? Tell your classroom teacher which page it was and what happened. Your teacher can help you get the content in another way.
